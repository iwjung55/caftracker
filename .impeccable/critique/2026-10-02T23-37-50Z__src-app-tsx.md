## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for this product, not a reskin. The strip-chart recorder sets the information architecture: the paper itself answers the three product questions (value at the pen, value at bed against the ruled target, the "NO MORE DRIP COFFEE TONIGHT" flag), and the line grammar encodes the model (solid = happened, dashed = projection, band = half-life uncertainty, pencil = not yet logged). Keycaps that stamp event marks, an archive filed as paper strips, a needle on a ruled scale instead of a ring — a generic habit tracker could not lift this. It slips back to generic in four places: the composer (stock chips, input, button), Calibrate (a plain settings column that never shows the instrument it calibrates), the "PEN 1" plate (costume on a one-pen instrument), and the night chart, where bezel #050707, housing #101413 and paper #0d1311 nearly merge and the framed-instrument read disappears.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI on markup (`src/App.tsx`, `src/components`, `index.html`): exit 0, **0 findings**. An extra scan of `src/styles` gave 5 advisory `design-system-color` findings — all **false positives**: rgba values inside box-shadows documented in DESIGN.md's Shadow Vocabulary (lamp ring ×2, key well ×2, snackbar). The key-well shadow is missing from the sidecar's `extensions.shadows` (sidecar gap). Browser overlay (run on a Vite dev server, because the production build's CSP `script-src 'self'` blocked injection — confirmed by a `securitypolicyviolation` event): **4 real line-length issues** — the notice (~90 chars/line), chart caption (~110, no measure cap), archive legend (~94, no cap), footer (~102; 90ch at 12px allows >100 chars). `tiny-text` on 11.5px chart flags is a documented choice, but the design review independently flagged 10.5–11px chart type for low-vision users, so treat it as **agreement, not a false positive**. False positives: `em-dash-overuse` (13 "—" are empty-day placeholders in the archive), `cramped-padding` on `.btn` (fixed 40px height), `dark-glow` (the documented status lamp). One real drift: `.channel__note--warn::before` glows without the documented 2px lamp ring.

**Visual overlays:** Injection succeeded on a temporary dev tab, but that tab and its servers were closed afterwards; no user-visible overlay remains.

## Overall Impression

A real instrument with a point of view; the arm-then-commit preview is the best thing in the product. The biggest opportunity: that preview is weakest exactly where logging happens — on the phone, in the evening.

## What's Working

1. **The line grammar encodes the model, not decoration.** Solid / dashed / band / pencil are separable by weight and dash, not just colour; bedtime, target and last-cup rules share one paper, so one glance answers now / bed / last cup.
2. **Arm, then commit.** One tap ghosts the effect on the chart, all four readout channels and the meter; a second tap logs; the re-plot wipe marks only user-caused change. A two-tap reflex without blind logging.
3. **Honest about uncertainty, and every drawn number is also stated.** "≈" throughout, the half-life band, sourced help ("No study sets a safe level"), a live sentence in Calibrate; the chart is focusable with arrow-key scrubbing and a polite live region, and each archive strip has a spoken summary.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] On a phone, the preview — the core promise — is not visible at the moment of decision**
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md says logging happens on the phone; the signature interaction is weakest exactly there, especially in the evening when bedtime is near "now".
- **What:** At 375px, arming smooth-scrolls the composer into view (EventDeck.tsx `useEffect` gated on `max-width: 899px`), pushing the chart off the top; the readout with "65 → 95 mg" / "None after this" sits below the composer; on 331px paper the ghost label "→ ≈95 mg" collides with "≈105 mg now"; the armed key wraps to "Tap to log 64 / mg"; the two-tap hint is `display:none` at ≤600px.
- **Fix:** Drop the auto-scroll at ≤600px; add a one-line decision strip in the composer ("Bed 65 → 95 mg · over 30 mg target · Today 270 → 317"); collapse mg/servings/when behind "Adjust"; stack or shorten colliding pen/bed/ghost labels in RecorderChart.tsx when `|nowX − bedX|` is under a label width; keep a short hint.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

**[P1] Undo is a 7-second unpausable timer with no practical keyboard path**
- **Why it matters:** Fails WCAG 2.2.1 (timing adjustable); Undo is the only guard on one-tap Delete and second-tap logging. The reviewer's own Undo expired before it could be reached.
- **What:** App.tsx clears the toast after 7 000 ms regardless of hover/focus; after keyboard logging focus stays on the key with Undo ~15 tab stops away; after a row Delete focus falls to `<body>`.
- **Fix:** Pause on `pointerenter`/`focusin`, extend to 10–12 s; add Ctrl/Cmd+Z; leave a "Just now · Undo" affordance on the newest log row for a few minutes; move focus to the next row after Delete; confirm Undo ("Removed Espresso").
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] The preview shifts layout and rescales the chart under the pointer**
- **Why it matters:** Breaks the steady-instrument feel and causes misfires: hovering a key near its top edge pushes it away, cancelling the preview — a flicker loop — and the second-tap target moves.
- **What:** Hovering Pre-workout moved `.keys` 710 → 731px because the Today note wraps to two lines in the 300px readout; the ghost forces a y-axis rescale (250 → 400), shrinking the real trace mid-comparison.
- **Fix:** Reserve two lines for `.channel__note` (or shorten the warning to "Over 400 mg reference"); fix the recorder row height; keep `yMax` stable while previewing and clip the ghost with an "↑ 320 mg" overflow marker (or animate the rescale).
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

**[P2] The evening state is a dead end, and the cutoff talks about the wrong drink**
- **Why it matters:** "Every number answers: should I, and when?" From mid-afternoon the answer is always "no" in amber, with nothing offered instead — an instrument that only refuses gets opened less.
- **What:** `CutoffChannel` (Readout.tsx) always computes for the usual drink, so arming black tea still reads "Last drip coffee for tonight: None after this".
- **Fix:** When a drink is armed, compute its own cutoff ("Last black tea: passed 4:10 PM"); in the `over` state state the largest dose that still fits ("≈20 mg still fits — half a green tea") or when tomorrow's first cup is fine.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Housekeeping wears the alarm colour; the disclaimer appears three times**
- **Why it matters:** "Back up your log" in Alarm Amber on every visit competes with the real physiological warning; on mobile it shrinks to an 8px colour-only dot with a 24×32 target.
- **Fix:** Lamp dot + Ink 2 text for housekeeping, Alarm Amber reserved for intake warnings; keep a visible "Back up" word and a 44px target on mobile; drop the footer disclaimer once the notice is acknowledged.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** No shortcuts for keys (1–6) or Undo; reaching keys by Tab passes nav, lamp, "Got it" and the chart. Escape doesn't disarm from the armed key. Keys reorder by 30-day frequency, so muscle memory breaks and nothing can be pinned. The picker's 23 drinks have no typeahead, and a picked drink has no key to tap twice.

**Sam (accessibility):** Arming announces only "Log Espresso, 64 mg, pressed" — the bedtime consequence (the whole point) isn't in a live region. 7 s Undo, unreachable by keyboard; focus lost after Delete. Mobile lamp conveys "needs backup" by amber vs green alone. Chart type is 10.5–11px condensed (detector agrees); row actions are 32px tall. Done well: chart scrubbing with a polite live region and spoken archive summaries.

**Casey (mobile, one hand, interrupted):** Arming scrolls the chart away and the readout sits below the composer — the decision is blind. The first-run notice takes ~40% of the first screen; keys start at y≈666 of 812. Again / Edit / Delete are 32px tall and 4px apart, with Delete beside Edit and only 7 s of Undo. The When control wraps at 375px ("Earlier…" breaks onto its own row). The snackbar wraps to three lines and covers the readout.

## Minor Observations

- Line length (detector): notice ~90 chars, chart caption ~110, archive legend ~94, footer ~102 — cap measures at ~70ch.
- `.channel__note--warn::before` glow lacks the documented 2px lamp ring; key-well shadow missing from the sidecar's `extensions.shadows`.
- Preview values drop "≈" ("≈65 → 105 mg"); "Tap to log" says "tap" to mouse and keyboard users.
- On compact paper the scale-pointer triangle overlaps the "100" axis label.
- Night chart: sleep region #18221e is lighter than paper #0d1311, so "night" reads brighter than day.
- Calibrate: sliders lack min/max labels; Reset to defaults and Remove are unguarded; no save confirmation; the evidence section's top rule is shorter than the others.
- Desktop lower deck: a 4-row log beside a 14-row archive leaves a large dead area.
- "4 drinks · 400 mg reference" repeats "4 logged".
- Storage-unavailable state shows no recovery step; picker items don't ghost on hover like keys.

## Questions to Consider

1. Logging happens on the phone, but the phone is where the preview is least visible. What would a phone-first armed state look like, with the chart pinned and the keys sliding up over the readout?
2. After 4 PM the instrument says "no" to everything. Should it answer "what *can* I still have?" (a half dose, green tea, decaf), or is the refusal the honest product?
3. Does "Pen 1" earn its place today? When sleep or mood becomes pen 2, it won't share the mg axis. Does the one-paper recorder survive the "built to hold more than caffeine" principle?
4. Calibrate changes the chart's physics but never shows the chart. Should calibration happen on the paper itself, by dragging the bedtime rule and the target line?
